TechVruk Internship Program - Company Knowledge Base
=====================================================

## About TechVruk
TechVruk is an innovative AI-first technology company founded in 2021, headquartered in Bangalore, India.
Our mission is to democratize artificial intelligence for businesses of all sizes across South Asia and beyond.
We specialize in building custom AI solutions, LLM-powered products, and intelligent automation pipelines.

## Our Core Products
1. VrukMind - An enterprise conversational AI platform built on top of large language models.
2. DataSense - A real-time analytics dashboard powered by ML anomaly detection.
3. AutoFlow - A no-code AI workflow builder that lets non-technical teams automate processes.

## Internship Program Details
- The TechVruk Summer Internship runs for 3 months (June to August).
- Interns are placed in one of four tracks: AI/ML, Backend Engineering, Frontend Engineering, or Product Design.
- Stipend for AI/ML track: INR 20,000 per month.
- All interns receive a MacBook Pro for the internship duration.
- The program ends with a Demo Day where interns present their projects to the leadership team.
- Top performers are offered Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) for full-time roles.

## Technical Stack Used at TechVruk
- Backend: Python (FastAPI, LangChain, LangGraph), Node.js
- Frontend: React.js, Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- AI/ML: Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT-4, HuggingFace models, FAISS, Pinecone
- Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Docker, Kubernetes
- Databases: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis

## Company Culture & Values
- We believe in "Ship Fast, Learn Faster" — an iterative build culture.
- Every Friday is dedicated to internal hackathons or tech talks (we call it "Vruk Fridays").
- Remote-first policy with optional coworking in our Bangalore office.
- We support open-source contributions and encourage engineers to publish research.

## HR & Policies
- Leave Policy: 12 annual leaves, 6 sick leaves per year for full-time employees.
- Interns are entitled to 2 casual leaves per month.
- Medical insurance is provided for all full-time employees (not interns).
- Performance reviews happen every 6 months.
- The HR point of contact for interns is hr-interns@techvruk.com.

## Interview & Hiring Process
- Round 1: Online coding test (Data Structures + Python basics)
- Round 2: Technical deep-dive (ML concepts, system design)
- Round 3: Culture fit interview with the team lead
- Offer letters are rolled out within 5 business days of the final round.
